Our Journey

The Story

Chapter I

The McFleet Era

It started on a Minecraft Discord server called McFleet. One night, I was just chatting away — and she suddenly appeared out of nowhere.

Anchal

Why are you still awake? Go get some sleep! 😤

...but she was online at that exact same time too 😂

That's how it began. We talked, we laughed, we enjoyed everything. She changed the culture of our entire crew — taught us how to be respectful, how to speak with kindness, and how to treat everyone well, even people who acted like our enemies.

Because of her, we don't just say "bye" anymore. We all say:

"Bye, take care, radhe radhe."

Chapter II

The Wammbu Gang

She invited me to another server called Wammbu Gang, where she was a MOD. I became a Trial MOD. This is where our friendship got its real test.

We had a little friction — a minor fight. I actually stepped down and resigned from my role. But instead of letting it break us, we talked it out. We fixed everything.

Our bond came back even stronger. I got my MOD role back. That moment proved something important:

This friendship is real.

Chapter III

Building Demonix

Then Yash suggested: "Why don't we build our own server?"

So we did. We named it Demonix. Vyntrix and I became the Developers, but Anchal — she stepped up as the ADMIN. She poured her absolute 100% into the soul and aesthetics of our new home.

From designing every channel name, to creating the banners, to setting the entire beautiful vibe — she built the identity of Demonix. We wrote the code, but she gave it a heartbeat.

Due to some issues, we eventually had to leave that server. But what we built there — that feeling, that bond — none of that left.
